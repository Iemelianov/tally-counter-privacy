Tally Counter — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

Tally Counter ("the app") is a simple counter for Android, published by Anton Iemelianov.

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or device data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no network access (it does not request the Internet permission or any other permission).

Data stored on your device

The current counter value is saved only on your device so it is still there the next time you open the app. It never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
